Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select places to visit?

You can choose destinations from our curated lists of beaches, religious places, and other tourist spots directly on the homepage.

Is this service free?

Yes, using Tap and Tour to browse and plan your travel routes is completely free.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Absolutely! After selecting your desired locations, our tool will help generate a custom summary for your travel plan.

Do I need to sign up to use the site?

While you can browse destinations without an account, signing up unlocks features like saving itineraries, receiving recommendations, and accessing exclusive travel deals.

What regions are currently covered by Tap and Tour?

We currently focus on travel in Maharashtra, including Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Lonavala, and coastal regions. We're continuously expanding to include more destinations across India.

Can I book accommodations or transport through the site?

We partner with trusted travel agencies and hotel providers. You’ll be redirected to verified platforms to complete bookings, ensuring security and quality service.

Is Tap and Tour mobile-friendly?

Yes! Our website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, so you can plan on the go.
